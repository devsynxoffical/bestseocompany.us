What it is

Link Building, explained.

Link building is the work of getting other websites to link to yours. Google uses links to discover pages and as one signal of trust. A good link comes from a real site in a related field, sits inside relevant content, and sends real readers your way. A link from a local newspaper or industry publication is worth far more than many links from low-quality sites.

Google's spam policies forbid link schemes. That includes buying links that pass ranking credit, private blog networks (PBNs), excessive link swaps, and automated link programs. These can lead to manual actions or to Google ignoring the links. We only use white-hat methods: digital PR, useful resources, expert commentary, and partnerships.

Link Building questions.
