What it is

Generative Engine Optimization, explained.

Generative engine optimization, or GEO, is about visibility in AI tools that write answers, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI Mode. These tools draw on what they learned in training and on live web results. Both depend on what the web says about you. Brands that are mentioned often, in the right context, on trusted sites tend to show up more.

AEO and GEO overlap heavily, and many people use the terms interchangeably. In our work, AEO focuses on answering specific questions on your own pages. GEO looks wider, at your brand's footprint across the web: reviews, press, directories, comparison articles, and community discussions. Original data and research help too, because other sites cite it. No one can control what a model says, so we focus on the inputs.
