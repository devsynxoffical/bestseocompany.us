What it is

Local SEO, explained.

Local SEO helps your business appear when people nearby search for your services, especially in the Google map pack.

It combines your Google Business Profile, reviews, local citations, location pages and website signals that show Google where you work and what you do. It is the core of our SEO services for small business.

Why local SEO matters for small businesses

Most local searches lead to a call or visit within a day. If you are not in the top three map results, many customers will never see you. Local SEO puts you where buying decisions happen.
