What you get.

Instant SEO audit

A live check of one page's technical and on-page basics, from status codes and HTTPS to titles, headings, and structured data.

Backlink report

Your referring domains, anchor text, most-linked pages, new and lost links, and a review for risky link patterns.

Traffic estimate

Estimated organic traffic, top ranking keywords and pages, and a comparison with competitors you name.

Ranking report

Google US positions for your keywords, nationally or for a city, plus map pack positions and SERP features.

Plain-English notes

Each result explains what it means and why it matters, so you can act on it without SEO jargon.

No account needed

The tools are free to use. You don't need to sign up or install anything.

Which tool should you start with?

Start with the SEO Audit Tool if you want a quick look at a page's health. It catches common problems like missing titles, noindex tags, and missing structured data.

Use the backlink, traffic, and rank reports when you want a wider view. They show who links to you, how much search traffic you may get, and where your keywords rank on Google.

What free tools can and can't tell you

Free tools are a good first check. They don't replace a full audit of every page, and third-party traffic data is always an estimate. For your own site, Google Search Console and GA4 are the source of truth.

If you want a deeper review, request our free 40-point audit. A person on our team looks at your whole site, your local presence, and your links.
