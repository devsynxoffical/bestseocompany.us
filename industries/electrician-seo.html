Why electrical contractors find SEO hard.

Every industry has its own search problems. These are the ones we solve first for electrical contractors.

Emergency searches go to the map pack

Someone with sparking outlets or no power will not scroll far. They call from the map pack. If your Google Business Profile is thin, has few recent reviews or lists the wrong hours, a competitor gets that call.

Residential and commercial work look different

A homeowner looking for a ceiling fan install and a facility manager looking for a three-phase service upgrade search in different ways. One generic services page can't rank for both. Each type of work needs its own page.

New services move fast

EV charger installs, whole-home generators, solar tie-ins and smart panels are growing search topics. Electricians who publish clear pages on these early tend to hold those rankings. Late movers have to outrank them.

Trust and licensing questions

Electrical work is dangerous and regulated, so people check licenses, insurance and reviews before they call. A site that hides your license number or uses stock photos gives them a reason to keep looking.

Ranking first next door to your office isn't enough. Customers search from every neighborhood you serve, and the map pack changes block by block. We track your position across a grid of points, then work on the areas where you're invisible.

Our electrician SEO playbook.

The order we work in for electrical contractors. Each step builds on the one before it.

  1. 01

    Service page for each job type

    We build dedicated pages for panel upgrades, rewiring, EV chargers, generators, lighting, troubleshooting and commercial work. Each page explains the job, the permit process and what affects cost in your area.

  2. 02

    Google Business Profile tuned for electricians

    We set "Electrician" as your primary category, add every service, define your service area and keep hours accurate, including after-hours availability. We post job photos and set up a review request after each completed job.

  3. 03

    Location pages for the towns you cover

    We create pages for your main towns using real local details, such as common older-home wiring issues, local permit offices and jobs you have done there. No copy-and-paste city swaps.

  4. 04

    Citations and trade links

    We clean up listings on Yelp, BBB, Nextdoor, Angi and data aggregators, then earn links from supplier and manufacturer installer directories, local associations and community sponsorships.
