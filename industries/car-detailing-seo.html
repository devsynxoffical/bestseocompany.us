Why car detailing businesses find SEO hard.

Every industry has its own search problems. These are the ones we solve first for car detailing businesses.

Mobile detailers lack a storefront

Mobile businesses often hide their address, which changes how they rank in the map pack. They need a service-area profile and strong town pages. Without those, they're invisible outside their home zip code.

Car washes crowd the results

Searches for "car detailing near me" often show express washes and gas station washes. Customers looking for real detailing have to dig. Clear categories and service pages help separate you.

High-value services need explaining

Ceramic coatings and paint correction cost much more than a wash. Customers want to understand the difference before they book. Thin pages don't justify the price.

Results are visual

Detailing sells on before-and-after shots. Many detailers post them on Instagram but not on their site or profile. Google can't use photos it never sees.

See it on the map

Win the map pack across your whole area.

Ranking first next door to your office isn't enough. Customers search from every neighborhood you serve, and the map pack changes block by block. We track your position across a grid of points, then work on the areas where you're invisible.

Our car detailing SEO playbook.

The order we work in for car detailing businesses. Each step builds on the one before it.

  1. 01

    Set up the right local profile

    We set up a storefront or service-area profile depending on how you work. Categories, services, booking links and photos come next.

  2. 02

    Build service pages that sell

    Pages for full details, interior, ceramic coating, paint correction, PPF and fleet work. Each explains the process, how long it lasts and what affects price.

  3. 03

    Show the work everywhere

    We move your best before-and-after photos onto your site and profile with short captions. Galleries by service help customers picture their own car.

  4. 04

    Cover your service area

    Town pages for the areas you drive to or pull customers from. Reviews that mention those areas reinforce each page.
