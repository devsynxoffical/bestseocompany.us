Why restaurants find SEO hard.

Every industry has its own search problems. These are the ones we solve first for restaurants.

Third-party sites outrank your own

Yelp, DoorDash, Tripadvisor and OpenTable often rank above a restaurant's own website. Those platforms take a cut of every order. Getting your site and profile to the top keeps more of that revenue in house.

Menus are often PDFs or images

A menu uploaded as a scanned PDF or photo is hard for Google to read. That means you miss searches for specific dishes like "birria tacos" or "gluten free pizza." An HTML menu fixes this.

Wrong hours cost real customers

Holiday hours, seasonal closures and kitchen cutoffs change often. When Google shows the wrong time, people show up to a locked door and leave a bad review. Keeping listings in sync across platforms takes routine effort.

Reviews move quickly and publicly

Restaurants collect reviews faster than most businesses, both good and bad. Unanswered complaints sit at the top of your profile for everyone to see. Response habits matter as much as review volume.

Our restaurants SEO playbook.

The order we work in for restaurants. Each step builds on the one before it.

  1. 01

    Clean up every listing

    We audit your Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Yelp and delivery listings for matching name, address, phone and hours. Then we set the right cuisine categories and attributes like outdoor seating or takeout.

  2. 02

    Rebuild the menu as real content

    We turn your menu into crawlable HTML with dish names, short descriptions and dietary notes. We add Menu schema so Google can understand it.

  3. 03

    Drive direct orders and bookings

    We make your own ordering link and reservation button the most visible action on your profile and site. That moves traffic away from commission-based apps where possible.

  4. 04

    Build a review and photo routine

    We set up simple ways to ask happy guests for reviews and a weekly habit of replying. We also add fresh dish and dining room photos so your profile looks current.
