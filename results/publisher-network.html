Case study · 3 months, Jul to Oct 2022

Publisher network: 3.45M clicks and 421M impressions in 3 months

Google Search Console reports from client projects delivered by our partner agency, shared with permission. Client names are hidden. Every figure is shown exactly as Google reports it.

Clicks3.45M
Impressions421M
CTR0.8%
Avg. position14.4

What did this site get from Google?

From July to October 2022 this publisher network earned 3.45 million clicks from 421 million Google impressions. That is roughly 37,500 clicks a day across a very large set of queries, with a 0.8 percent click-through rate and an average position of 14.4.

What the report shows.

Read straight from the numbers and the chart. Where we do simple math, like clicks per day, we say so.

01

Scale: about 37,500 clicks a day

3.45M clicks over roughly 92 days averages about 37,500 clicks a day from Google.

02

421 million impressions

At this size a site ranks for an enormous number of searches, many of them on page two, which is why the CTR (0.8 percent) is low while total clicks are huge.

03

A clear weekly rhythm

The chart shows a regular weekly cycle in clicks, with peaks and dips repeating every week, typical of news and content publishers.

04

Rising impressions

Impressions trend upward through August and September compared with July.

How to read this report

Clicks
How many times someone clicked through to the site from Google Search.
Impressions
How many times the site appeared in a searcher's results, even if they didn't scroll to it.
CTR
Click-through rate: clicks divided by impressions. It shows how often people chose this site when they saw it.
Avg. position
The site's average ranking across every query it appeared for. 1 to 10 is roughly page one.

What we can share

This report comes from a client project delivered by our partner agency and is published with permission. The client's name and the specific work are kept private, so we show the result exactly as Google reports it and nothing we can't verify.
